# SFO Schedule

SFO (skolefritidsordning) runs outside school hours — typically 06:30–08:00 and 13:00–17:00. It is staff-rota-based, not lesson-based. There are no fixed subjects or classes; staff cover the SFO facility as a whole (or by loose age group).

Key differences from a class timetable:
- Schedule unit is a **shift block** (staff + time range), not a lesson slot (fag + klasse + lokale)
- No course/room/class linkage
- Coverage is the core constraint: enough adults per child count (minimum staffing threshold)
- SFO wants flexible **week plans** — events, food days, outdoor play, trips — that differ week to week (not a fixed repeating timetable)
