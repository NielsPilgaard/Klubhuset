# School-based pricing, not per-student

**Status**: Accepted

## Decision

Pricing is a flat monthly fee per school. Tiers are differentiated by what costs us more to provide (storage quota, support level, advanced features) — not by student count or school size.

## Reason

Per-student pricing is unpredictable and schools dislike it — their student count fluctuates and they want a fixed budget line. A flat fee is simpler, more transparent, and aligns with the product's simplicity-first principle. Student count does not meaningfully drive our infrastructure costs.
