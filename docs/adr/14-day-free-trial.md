# 14-day free trial replaces free tier

**Status**: Accepted

## Decision

New schools get a 14-day free trial with full Basis access. No credit card required to start. There is no permanently free tier.

## Reason

A free tier with limited features adds product complexity (tier gating, upgrade prompts, feature flags) and provides a worse first impression. A full-access trial lets schools evaluate the real product. Schools that find value will convert; schools that don't were never going to pay regardless of how long they had a free tier.
