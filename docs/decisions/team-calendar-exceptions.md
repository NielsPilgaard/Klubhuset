# Full team calendar with exception support

**Status**: Accepted

## Decision

Team schedules use a weekly recurrence pattern (day + time + location) as the baseline. Admins and trænere can cancel or reschedule individual sessions (exceptions). When a session is cancelled or rescheduled, all team members receive an email notification.

## Reason

Training cancellations and rescheduling are routine in Danish sports clubs (weather, holidays, hall unavailability). A simple text field would force manual communication every time. Exception support reduces admin overhead and keeps members informed automatically.
