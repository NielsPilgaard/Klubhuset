# White-label is paid-only

**Status**: Accepted

## Decision

Free tier clubs are served on `klubhuset.dk/{slug}` with Klubhuset branding visible. White-label (club-only branding) is a paid feature from Forening tier upward.

## Reason

Primary upgrade lever. Free tier is a try-before-you-buy, not a full product. Clubs that care about their brand identity will upgrade.
