# Free tier is fully bypassed from payments

**Status**: Accepted

## Decision

Free tier clubs connect their own MobilePay Business account. Klubhuset never touches their money. The platform displays the club's MobilePay Betalingslink and QR code. Admin manually marks members as paid in the dashboard.

## Reason

Eliminates payment facilitator regulatory requirements, PCI compliance burden, and payout logic for free tier. Clear upgrade incentive: want platform-managed payments? Upgrade.
