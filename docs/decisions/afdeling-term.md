# "Afdeling" term kept; one afdeling = one sport

**Status**: Accepted

## Decision

The term "afdeling" is used throughout the UI. One afdeling represents one sport (e.g. fodbold, badminton, gymnastics, dans). The term is introduced with a short explanatory subtitle on first encounter rather than being renamed or hidden.

## Reason

Danish clubs already know and use the word "afdeling". Renaming it (e.g. to "sport" or "klub") would either be too vague or invent new jargon. The better fix is to explain it once clearly, not to replace it.
